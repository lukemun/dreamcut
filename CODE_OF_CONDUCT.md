# Code of conduct

DreamCut follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else you represent the project.

In short: be kind and specific, assume good intent, and keep criticism about the work. Harassment,
personal attacks and sharing someone's media or private information without consent are not accepted.

Report a problem privately through [dreamcut.io](https://dreamcut.io). The maintainer reviews every report,
keeps the reporter's identity private, and may remove content or block accounts that break this code.
