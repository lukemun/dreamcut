# Inspector sample photographs

The two landscape photographs provide a consistent, recognizably different A/B pair for comparing native transitions. Each is reframed to a 360 × 640 preview.

- **landscape-a.png**: NPS / Neal Herbert, *Yellowstone Lake from Lake Butte Overlook*, 2014. US public domain. [Source and license](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Yellowstone_Lake_from_Lake_Butte_Overlook_(027f0dda-1dd8-b71b-0b83-b097b77d44e5).jpg).
- **landscape-b.png**: NPS / A. Falgoust, *Sunset over Yellowstone Lake*, 2020. US public domain. [Source and license](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sunset_over_Yellowstone_Lake.jpg).
- **a.png** (previous transition source): Christopher Campbell, *Brunette woman portrait*, 2015. CC0. [Source and license](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Brunette_woman_portrait_(Unsplash).jpg).
- **b.png / background.png** (subject-effect background): NPS / Jacob W. Frank, *Hiker and Raven on Specimen Ridge*, 2017. US public domain, US National Park Service. [Source and license](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hiker_and_Raven_on_Specimen_Ridge_(portrait)_(37038812242).jpg).
- **subject.png**: AI-assisted background removal derived from the CC0 portrait. This is an illustrative still cutout, not evidence of moving subject tracking. The original photograph remains the authority for the transition source image.

The recorded previews use the production compositor, with fifteen treatment frames at 30 fps and eight context frames on either side. The manifest records their source and output hashes. The `--photos` recorder option substitutes this public sample set in memory only; it does not edit or approve the sample project's working timeline.
