# audio-balance — evolution log

## Purpose
Diagnose and improve dialogue, music, ambience, fades, loudness, and voice clarity, measuring before changing levels. Scope: mix decisions proposed through proposals; it never silently replaces source media, and voice enhancement stays a reversible derived source with the original retained. Good looks like: intelligible speech, no clipping, gentle fades, transparent ducking under dialogue, and intentional room tone preserved.
