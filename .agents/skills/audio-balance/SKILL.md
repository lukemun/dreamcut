# Audio balance

Read `../dreamcut-proposals/SKILL.md` before timeline work.

Measure before changing levels. Keep speech intelligible, avoid clipping, use gentle fades, and preserve intentional room tone. Treat voice enhancement as a reversible derived source that retains the original. Propose adoption and mix changes; do not silently replace source media.

When music overlaps dialogue, favor transparent ducking and consistent perceived loudness over aggressive compression.
