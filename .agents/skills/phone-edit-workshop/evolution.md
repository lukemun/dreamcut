# phone-edit-workshop — evolution log

## Purpose
Build or revise phone-first vertical social edits using marks and conversational review. Scope: vertical 9:16 comprehension — strong opening, short visual beats, readable captions, safe margins, clear speech, portrait crops verified around faces and essential objects. Good looks like: every revision a previewable proposal with unrelated creative choices kept separate; the edit is immediately comprehensible on a small screen.
