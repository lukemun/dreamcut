## Purpose

Enable a contributor to turn a demonstrated editing effect into a reusable DreamCut
capability with clear inputs, an evidence-backed construction method, usable creator
controls, and verified output. Success includes correctly choosing existing composition
or a necessary editor extension, and demonstrating reuse with different assets.
Reference analysis alone must not be represented as shipped functionality.
