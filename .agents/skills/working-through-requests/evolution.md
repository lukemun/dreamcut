# working-through-requests — evolution log

## Purpose
Be the default routine whenever the creator asks for several changes to an existing edit: capture each ask so it is visible under Up next, draft one to three small cards per pass, and draft each next pass from the timeline as the creator left it. Scope: pacing and routing only; card construction stays in dreamcut-proposals and the craft skills, and the routine never approves, rejects or applies anything. Good looks like: no dropped asks, never a pile of cards, and each pass reflecting the creator's latest picks.

## 2026-10-25 — Written down as the default routine

- **Observation:** The creator found it bad when many proposals arrived at once, and asked for sound effects "in batches, not all at once", wanting this written down as the repeatable default they will teach others. The rules disagreed: AGENTS.md asked for passes of one to three cards, while dreamcut-proposals said to draft every recorded ask, and every wave step, up front.
- **Decision:** One routine: record each ask (Up next is the queue), draft one to three cards per pass, stop for the creator's decisions, then draft the next pass from the current timeline. Queued asks stay open so they remain visible; dreamcut-proposals now points here instead of drafting everything up front.
- **Verification:** Applied by hand on a fly-in project: three recorded sound asks, two drafted as the first pass, one left under Up next. Not yet measured on fresh agent runs.

## 2026-10-26 — Get each card right before staging

- **Observation:** In one weekly-recap session, 97 of 155 cards were superseded by the agent's own redrafts, and only one was a creator rejection. About 60 came from re-sending a 13-card visuals batch after each early layout correction, and 43% of staging calls were refused by validators: schema mistakes, overlap, the dial limit, and cuts inside words. The creator: "redrafting is not noise, why wasn't it done right the first time".
- **Decision:** Step 3 says to stage one card first when the direction is open, look at its frames before presenting it, check what the validators would refuse before staging, and never invent on-screen wording. Step 5 says a direction correction redrafts the queue, and a revision keeps the dials of the card it replaces.
- **Verification:** Documentation change; not yet measured on fresh agent runs. Measure by superseded-to-applied ratio and refusal rate per session.
