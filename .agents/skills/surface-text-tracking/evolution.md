# Surface text tracking — evolution

## Purpose

Attach native editable text to a moving planar surface with correct source timing,
visibility and perspective. Own preparation, targeted diagnosis and visual verification;
leave masks to rotoscope and general lettering to video-typography. Success is editable
text that stays attached through the reviewed motion, with evidence and limitations
separated from job completion or delivery claims.

## 2026-10-03 — initial procedure

- **Observation:** Repeated corrections involved early disappearance, wobble during a
  surface turn and playback-only layer separation despite plausible paused frames.
- **Decision:** Add a dedicated workflow separating corner tracking, source-clock/parent
  mapping and presented-frame synchronization. Inspect entrance and departure as well
  as the clearest reference frame; preserve good intervals when correcting failures.
- **Verification:** Tool signatures, attachment schema and interpolation/parent limits
  checked against current implementation. Matching, neighboring and missing-input cases
  reviewed as written walkthroughs. Existing private synthetic renderer receipts support
  the capability, not execution of this newly authored skill. New-footage efficiency and
  correction-count improvement remain unmeasured.
- **Validation result:** Skill catalog/purpose and surface-track tests passed (9 total);
  new relative links resolve. The new catalog card was not visually inspected. No new
  media trial or broad repository pass is claimed.
