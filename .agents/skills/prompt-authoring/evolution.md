# prompt-authoring — evolution log

## Purpose

Turn creator intent into concise, grounded instructions that a specific agent or
generation integration can execute and assess. Success preserves the requested
scope, uses available inputs honestly, and supplies observable acceptance criteria.
Prompt writing does not establish media understanding or authorize execution.

## 2026-10-01 — Updated: observable fixed-framing direction

- **Observation:** A portrait motion quality revision introduced a progressive
  push-in despite the submitted prompt specifying a locked eye-level camera and
  unchanged framing. Existing guidance separated camera and subject motion but
  did not give fixed-framing invariants a concrete example. This is observed model
  noncompliance; omission of camera direction was not the cause established by evidence.
- **Decision:** Clarified the video craft reference with stationary camera position,
  fixed focal length/distance, prohibited camera moves and stable size/margins.
  Preserved intentional subject approach as a counterexample. No provider settings,
  default camera aesthetic or guarantee of model compliance were introduced.
- **Verification:** Baseline prompt and all-frame review were compared in a private
  project-local review packet. Skill discovery checks and written routing walkthroughs
  validate the documentation; a corrected generation has not yet been run, so improved
  camera compliance remains unproven.
