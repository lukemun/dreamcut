# talking-video-editing — evolution log

## Purpose
Edit presenter, interview, vlog, or direct-to-camera video, coordinating pacing, captions, supporting visuals, audio, and delivery. Scope: the coordinator — each concern delegated to its specialist skill; initial assembly deferred to editorial-assembly's presenter-first gate. Good looks like: a clean dialogue spine first, then layered finishing; the speaker's meaning, identity, and cadence protected; unrelated creative judgments independently reviewable while one coordinated scene's timing is judged together.
