# Talking video editing

Read `../open-edit-proposals/SKILL.md` before timeline work.

For initial assembly or a major presenter rebuild, follow the **Presenter-first standard and review gate** in `../editorial-assembly/SKILL.md`: selected speech edit → linked presenter appearance → source-bound captions → accepted presenter-only playback → supporting visuals. One source means one authoritative edit map, not one flattened file. Detached voice remains linked and must be the sole audible voice path. Do not treat processing chunks as separate editorial decisions. Targeted revisions need only reopen the affected foundation checks, not restart unrelated work.

Protect the speaker's meaning, identity, and cadence. Establish a clean dialogue spine first, then add captions, supporting visuals, music, and finishing. Use the narrow specialist skill for each concern: `cut-pacing`, `caption-craft`, `audio-balance`, `planned-shot-craft`, `visual-timing`, `spatial-composition`, `video-typography`, and `platform-video-delivery`.

Keep unrelated creative judgments independently reviewable, but keep the timing and transforms of one coordinated scene together so its combined result can be judged. Do not render unless the user separately asks for delivery.
