# explainer-visuals — evolution log

## Purpose
Give a talking video graphics that add to what is said: the data, names, scale, comparison or
mechanism behind each spoken idea, timed to the words, from real sources. Scope: deciding what
each graphic shows, planning beats from the transcript and the creator's marks, sourcing figures,
and the timing and review path; construction belongs to motion-graphics and its templates.
Good looks like: no graphic restates the spoken sentence; every figure is sourced on its card;
each beat is its own card, applied in small passes; motion lands on the words.
