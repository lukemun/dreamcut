# Planned shot craft

Read `../open-edit-proposals/SKILL.md` before timeline work.
Read `../visual-timing/SKILL.md` before timing supporting material, and `../spatial-composition/SKILL.md` whenever foreground actors coexist.

Give every supporting shot a narrative job: establish, prove, explain, hide a cut, reset attention, or close. Match the exact spoken idea, enter on a motivated phrase, and leave before the shot becomes stale. Prefer available authentic material over generic filler.

Plan a complete beat map and combined scene states before proposing individual elements. Planning is read-only. Timeline placement, timing, crop, and compositing are proposal operations.

## Learned refinements

- When a supporting visual represents a specifically spoken concept, align its first visible frame to that concept’s spoken word.
