# talking-head-cutout — evolution log

## Purpose
Create, refine, and audit source-synchronized moving presenter cutouts (SAM 2), especially Scratchpad-style talking heads. Scope: the person matte and its evidence — provenance via editor-media-preprocessing, compositing via video-compositing, timeline adoption via proposals with sync_speech_visual; chroma key only for genuinely even solid-color sources. Good looks like: the entire visible subject survives the full interval with hair, hands, and silhouette continuity as acceptance criteria; the matte audited on black, white, and checkerboard with per-second contact sheets; defects fixed in the matte, never concealed with blur.
