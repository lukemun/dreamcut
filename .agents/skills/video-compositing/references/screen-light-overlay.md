# Black-backed light overlays

Use this native procedure for a supplied portal, sparks or glow over a continuous
performance when the reference keeps the performer clear of the main light form.
It is a draft construction method; the first supplied-footage prototype has
unresolved formation-phase fidelity and no fresh rebuild or transfer certification.
For an actor crossing the portal, establish the foreground matte separately through
[object cutouts](object-cutouts.md); this basic route does not infer that occlusion.

## Inputs and measurement

Require the original performance and the actual light-overlay file, with provenance,
source hashes and ready preview/render packages through
[media intake](../../media-intake/SKILL.md). Keep each role separately editable.
Use the final requested lesson variant and distinguish its playback from tutorial
pauses, scrubbing, trials and narration. Record first spark, established form,
performer gesture and ending on their actual source/output clocks. A rounded total
duration in another editor is not an exact frame count.

Inspect the overlay's formation, established state and ending. Similar filenames or
one circular still do not establish animation identity. If the available supplied
file differs, record the adaptation and uncertainty before building. Fit the visible
light form: differently padded files can have different selection boxes while their
visible features need the same position. Keep black bounds and visible bounds distinct.
Use dense source-PTS inspection for a consequential phase question. Resampled analysis
proxies are navigation; retain their mapping and do not call proxy indices exact
original timestamps. Pattern correlation needs finite, nonzero template/window energy
and visual confirmation before it can support a match.

## Editable construction

Follow [proposal authoring](../../dreamcut-proposals/SKILL.md) and
[spatial composition](../../spatial-composition/SKILL.md). Use the existing workspace
authority for a first assembly and proposals after handoff. The complete scene has:

- one continuous original performer clip at the measured source window and rate;
- one separate source-backed light overlay above it on an adjacent visual lane;
- native `media_blend_mode: "screen"`, source rate/window, opacity and placement;
- only the applicable supplied audio, independently source-linked when present.

Confirm Screen in the live schema/runtime rather than assuming that a renderer enum
is exposed. Black keying changes the operation and is not an equivalent fallback.
The top-first declared lane order must include every active visual lane. Keep source
URLs as render authorities and consume the prepared packages for interactive preview.
Use the spatial tool's span result, but retain unknowns: no detected face or a blend
mode requiring pixel analysis does not prove performer clearance.

Measure the visible form against the scene and the performer's complete gesture.
When relation-based placement cannot express the reference registration, declare the
coordinate frame, measurement source and anchor through the supported fallback.
Inspect the combined pixels; a full black-backed source rectangle over the performer
does not itself establish visible occlusion after Screen blending.

Expose onset/lead-in, size, both position axes and opacity when useful. For a lead-in
that preserves the light animation's lifetime, bind the overlay start and the live
performance's ending to the same native hold delta. The existing `source_tail`
binding can preserve the performance's source clock and handles. Use complete trim
patches and the actual prepared source-duration authority; inspect the derived result
at both limits and the authored default. Keep source rate/window controls distinct:
changing rate independently can alter the light's lifetime and invalidate ending
alignment. Do not describe an untested cross-control combination as coupled.

When the performance must remain behind every overlay frame, declare the option's
explicit `coverage_constraints` relation from the created overlay operation to the
performance patch. The patch needs an explicit start/duration and a source-tail
hold that owns its source clock. This bounds Source in/out and Speed against the
supporting interval across the complete legal hold range; it does not extend the
performance or infer coupling from equal endings. Valid shorter/faster and shifted
source windows remain available. An ordinary independent overlay needs no relation.
Verify the live tool schema supports this contract before staging it, and inspect
combined source/rate edits, Reset and persistence as well as the individual sliders.

## Review and reuse

Activate the proposal's **Preview** before judging its combined picture. Selecting
the review card can leave the viewer on the untreated timeline. Confirm the active
preview state and inspect settled frames after a control edit. Test the relevant
endpoints, native Reset and persistence, then verify that saved tuning is restored.
Human Apply, Reject and history restoration remain the human's decisions.

Compare the entire setup, gesture, formation, established state and ending at normal
speed and matching offsets. Check actor integrity, glow over protected features,
grounding, scale, arc direction, spark density, black borders and the full ending.
Inspect difficult boundaries at native pixels. Preserve source marks/watermarks.
Decoded silence can establish source-audio preservation; a mixed narrated lesson does
not supply isolated SFX or prove soundtrack identity. Use
[sound design](../../sound-design/SKILL.md) for applicable supplied cues.

Keep technical decode/source-clock checks, sampled/native edge findings and actual
continuous sensory coverage separate. Follow the template-authoring learning loop for
a saved package, clean-input rebuild, independent grading and new-input transfer.
An editable first prototype or a silent output is not a completed reusable effect.
