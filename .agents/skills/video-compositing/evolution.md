## 2026-11-02 — Reusable compositing and source-clock lessons

- Keep fractional mask and feather precision consistent across renderer, inspector,
  proposal metadata, tuning and Reset; otherwise a valid authored value changes on edit.
- Retain an exhausted static plate with a bounded native trailing hold. Coverage
  validation and trim/speed dial bounds must reserve that held interval.
- Judge the complete object/hand silhouette when tracking a boundary; a median
  colored edge can cut off protruding fingers. Do not hold a terminal black frame.
- Preserve every essential action from the full tutorial, including rotation,
  enlargement and late cleanup. Keep reference-specific measurements in private evidence.
- Separate normal-speed performance from a continuous accelerated environment.
  Muted high-rate playback must not assign an unsupported audio-element rate.
- Center temporal sampling on the source clock, preserve frames and timestamps,
  and validate the native preparation in the original effect without changing its tuning.
  Corrected temporal lag alone does not establish freedom from doubled contours.
- Treat photo softness separately from velocity blur. Compensate source softness
  for camera enlargement while preserving both lettering and the keyed silhouette.
- A light overlay's source interval and final hold must remain covered by the
  supporting performance across the entire allowed timing-control range.
- Match supplied SFX occurrences to visible anchors; preserve linked timing when
  editing speed, layer placement and ending holds.
- Keep export, control, continuous-motion, listening, repeatability and transfer
  evidence separate. Reusable instructions must not contain private session receipts
  or promote sampled engineering checks into full sensory certification.

These are method and validation lessons. They make no claim that a particular
private reconstruction passed every acceptance gate.
