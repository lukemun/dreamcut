# dreamcut-proposals — evolution log

## Purpose
The mandatory path for agent edits to an existing working timeline: every agent change goes through a previewable proposal the human reviews, adjusts, approves, or rejects. Scope: inspection and mutation of timelines already on the working cut — inside a fresh assembly workspace, direct construction applies instead. The agent never approves or rejects. Good looks like: one independently reviewable beat per proposal; derived review rows computed from the patch (the agent authors rationale and chooses supported controls, never row descriptions); approval is atomic with before/after history snapshots. The first proposal supplies the working controls and useful ranges needed to reach the intended result, with likely adjustments prioritized and relevant secondary choices available. Success includes fewer follow-up requests and replacement proposals solely for missing dials, assessed with explicit audit-coverage limits.

## 2026-10-23 — Plain-words card text

## 2026-10-24 — Dials that errored while the creator tuned them

- **Observation:** The creator often hit errors while dragging a card's sliders; nothing checked that a dial still produces a preview at the ends of its slider.
- **Decision:** `proposal_create` sweeps every numeric dial to both ends of the slider the card draws and builds the preview there through the preview route's steps, main dials first within 3 s, and returns `dial_sweep` plus a warning per failing dial. The slider range now has one owner (`proposal-dial-range.ts`) shared by the card and the sweep. It warns rather than refuses because a first sweep of 47 pending cards found failures on 36, many from slider ranges that reach values the edit itself refuses (a start that overlaps a neighbour, a length shorter than its keyframes); those need range fixes, not dropped dials. Two real slider bugs it found are fixed: a working-range minimum a rounding error below the field minimum (audio fade, durations), and whole-number dials (Variation) whose ends fell between steps.
- **Verification:** Unit tests for the swept values and range snapping; read-only sweeps over existing pending cards before and after. Not yet measured on fresh agent runs; choice and colour dials are not swept.

## 2026-10-24 — A camera Duration dial that did nothing

- **Observation:** On the chalk push-in card, dragging the camera's Duration only lengthened the camera clip; its zoom keys stayed at fixed times, so Replay looked identical and the creator called it a critical error.
- **Decision:** A camera's duration override now scales its keyframes, so the move speeds up or slows down. The dial sweep compares what the preview shows at both slider ends (every non-camera clip's timing, sound and settings, plus where each picture sits at 25 moments across the playback range); a main dial with identical ends refuses the card (`dead_dials`), an advanced one warns. Slider ranges for Source in/out, Duration and Speed were narrowed so they never reach values the edit refuses (in after out, too short for the clip's keys, running into the next clip on the lane).
- **Verification:** Unit tests for camera keyframe scaling and swept values; read-only sweeps over 75 pending cards (dial failures fell from 156 to 17 after the range fixes). Not yet measured on fresh agent runs.
