# research-deliverable — evolution log

## Purpose
Produce research deliverables that are pure evidence: every factual claim inline-linked to a primary source, with no synthesis, spin, or product interpretation. Scope: the evidence layer only (raw captures, then the findings doc) — interpretation belongs in a separate application doc that cites it. Good looks like: every claim sourced at the point of claim, primary sources over secondary, quoting where exact wording matters; a cross-source pattern is interpretation and stays out of the deliverable.
