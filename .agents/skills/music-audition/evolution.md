# music-audition — evolution log

## Purpose
Find cataloged music passages and prepare playable comparisons for a scene, mood, or narrative turn. Scope: choosing and comparing music inside a current edit — catalog building without an edit belongs to music-catalog-ingestion. Good looks like: cue-level matching (mood tags, energy trajectory, turn offsets, duration, narration needs), contrasting editorial readings rather than similar filenames, measured loudness comparisons, and provisional judgments stated as provisional until heard against the edit.
