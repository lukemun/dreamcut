# music-direction — evolution log

## Purpose
Plan a section-led music arc for an edit, then coordinate cue auditions, continuity, and dialogue-first mixing. Scope: sections divided by narrative turns (not per shot), persisted as analysis/music-direction.json; playable comparisons delegated to music-audition. Good looks like: one musical arc across sections — continuing a bed, changing its intensity, returning to a motif, or using silence — never a jukebox of unrelated cues; dialogue stays readable; a small number of cohesive cues changing at meaningful narrative turns.
